What this is

A body is a tool, an identity is a key

English

Parazit is a third-person action/stealth game about a bodiless consciousness that jumps between enemy bodies in the middle of a fight. Every body you take gives you its identity, its access, its weapon and its function — along with its limits.

The core loop is not simply changing bodies; it is reading what each body is good for and spending it as a temporary resource.

Türkçe

Parazit, bedensiz bir bilincin çatışmanın ortasında düşman bedenlerine sıçradığı üçüncü şahıs bir aksiyon/gizlilik oyunu. Sıçradığın her beden sana kendi kimliğini, erişim yetkisini, silahını ve işlevini devreder — ve onunla birlikte kendi sınırlarını.

Oyunun çekirdeği beden değiştirmek değil; hangi bedenin ne işe yaradığını okuyup onu geçici bir kaynak olarak kullanmaktır.

How it plays

Three rules

Jump

Take a body within range. The shell you leave behind drops where it stands and stays there as evidence — who fell, and where, is a trail for whoever is looking for you.

Read

Every body opens a different door. A technician's clearance, a guard's weapon, the room a nurse can walk into. Being in the wrong place in the wrong body is how you get noticed.

Spend

Shells run out. The body you stay in wears down, gets recognised, gets hunted. Knowing when to leave matters as much as knowing where to go next.
